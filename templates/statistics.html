Section 19 • Important Statistical Rule: P-Value vs Bayesian Probability
Frequentist Paradigm
P-Value (Statistical Significance)

Definition: The probability of observing data at least as extreme as the sample data, assuming the null hypothesis ($H_0$) is true.

p < 0.05 → Evidence against the null hypothesis (Reject H0)
p ≥ 0.05 → Insufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis
Common Fallacy: A p-value does NOT mean the null hypothesis has a probability of being true, nor does it quantify an individual's disease chance.
Bayesian Paradigm
Bayesian Probability (Disease Risk)

Definition: The updated degree of belief in the patient's disease state conditioned on all observed clinical evidence:

P(Disease = 1 | Age, CP, BP, Chol, Thalach, Oldpeak, Exang) = 78.0%
Clinical Role: Bayesian probability provides an individual patient risk estimate by propagating evidence through a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG).
