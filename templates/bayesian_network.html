Learned Conditional Probability Distributions (CPDs)

Parameters estimated from patient training data via Maximum Likelihood Estimation. Exact posterior queries evaluate: $$P(\text{Disease} \mid \mathbf{e}) = \frac{\sum_{\mathbf{X} \setminus \{\text{Target}\}} \prod_{i} P(X_i \mid \text{Parents}(X_i))}{\sum_{\text{Target}} \sum_{\mathbf{X} \setminus \{\text{Target}\}} \prod_{i} P(X_i \mid \text{Parents}(X_i))}$$

P(Target | CP, Sex, Hemodynamics)

Target cardinality: 2 (0 = No Disease, 1 = Disease)

Conditioned on 7 simultaneous clinical risk factors.

P(CP | Age)

CP cardinality: 4 categories (Typical, Atypical, Non-anginal, Asymptomatic)

Conditioned on 3 age groups.

P(Cholesterol | Age) & P(BP | Age)

Hemodynamic cardinality: 3 categories each

Models biological degradation over patient lifespans.
