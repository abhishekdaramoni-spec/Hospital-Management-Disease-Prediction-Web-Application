Critical Distinction: Statistical Significance vs Bayesian Disease Probability
Frequentist p-value (e.g. p = 0.0001):

Measures the probability of obtaining test results at least as extreme as the observed cohort data, under the assumption that the null hypothesis $H_0$ is true. It does NOT measure the probability that the patient has a disease!

Bayesian Risk Probability (e.g. {{ prediction.disease_probability }}%):

Measures this specific patient's conditional posterior probability $P(\text{Disease} \mid \text{Patient Evidence})$ given their observed CP type, blood pressure, cholesterol, heart rate, and demographic profile under the learned DAG joint probability distribution.
